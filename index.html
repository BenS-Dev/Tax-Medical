What this estimate leaves out

  • It uses 2026 Manitoba rates and assumes the small business deduction applies (9% on the first $500,000 of active business income, 27% above).
  • Personal tax covers federal, Manitoba, CPP and optional EI.
  • Retained earnings are taxed again when paid out as dividends. That second layer isn't shown here.
  • RRSP contributions, other deductions and advanced strategies aren't included.
  • Talk to a tax professional before making decisions based on these figures.
